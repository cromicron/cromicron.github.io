Your workspace.
A private assistant.

Anna Workspace connects one owner's Google account to a personal assistant running on a private virtual machine. It is not a public sign-up service.

Mail

Read email and prepare drafts. Send messages when the owner requests them.

Files

Work with Drive files, Google Docs, Sheets and Slides through the owner's authorized account.

Calendar

Read and manage calendar information for tasks requested by the owner.

Access stays under the owner's control.

The owner chooses the Google permissions and the tasks Anna performs. OAuth credentials are stored in protected controller storage on the private virtual machine. Relevant task content may be processed by the configured AI provider.

The privacy page explains what data is used, how access can be revoked, and how task records and provider processing are handled.
