Privacy

Last updated · 16 October 2026

Anna Workspace is a private assistant integration for its account owner. It connects the dedicated Anna Google account to the owner's private assistant environment. It is not offered as a public service.

Google data used

Depending on the permissions granted and the task requested, the application can access the account identity, email messages and drafts, Drive files, Docs documents, Sheets spreadsheets, Slides presentations, and Calendar information. Google permissions can be broader than the operations exposed by the assistant's current tools.

Data is used to carry out the owner's requested assistant functions, such as reading information, preparing content, updating files or managing events. Sending email and other external actions require an owner instruction. Google data is not sold or used for advertising by this application.

Where processing happens

The private virtual machine connects to Google using OAuth. Relevant task instructions, retrieved content and results may be sent to the configured AI provider to perform the requested task. The configured AI providers are OpenAI and Anthropic (Claude). Which provider receives relevant task content depends on the model selected for the session. Provider processing, retention and account data controls are governed by the applicable provider terms and the owner's account settings; this page does not make an independent promise about provider model training or retention.

Google access credentials remain in protected controller storage on the virtual machine. They are not made available to the assistant's isolated worker workspace. The application does not intentionally include credentials in model prompts.

Task records and retention

Task history, logs, generated outputs and downloaded files may retain information used during a task. There is no fixed automatic deletion period promised by this application. The owner can request removal of local task records and files and manage provider-side data through the relevant provider's controls.

Revoking access

The owner can revoke Anna Workspace's Google access at Google Account connections. Revocation stops future authorized access but does not automatically delete previously created files, local records or provider-held data.

This website

These pages contain no application analytics, cookies, third-party scripts or externally loaded fonts. They are hosted by GitHub Pages, whose infrastructure may process technical request information under GitHub's privacy statement.

Contact and changes

Questions about this integration or requests concerning its local records can be sent to anna.cromicron@gmail.com. This policy should be updated before adding a materially different data use or AI provider.
