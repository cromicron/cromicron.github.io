Private, personal use

Anna Workspace is an experimental integration operated for its account owner's personal use. It is not a public sign-up service and does not charge users a fee.

Authorized tasks

The owner controls connected accounts, permissions and task instructions. Access must be limited to accounts and information the owner is authorized to use. A Google permission grant does not itself instruct the assistant to send messages, publish content or perform an unrelated task.

Review and availability

AI-generated results can contain mistakes. The owner should review results before relying on them or authorizing consequential actions. Availability depends on the private virtual machine, the configured AI service, Google APIs and valid account authorization.

Third-party services

Google, the configured AI provider and GitHub Pages provide separate services under their own applicable terms. This integration does not replace those terms. Data handling is described in the privacy policy.

Stopping use

The owner may stop using the integration and revoke Google access through Google Account connections. Revocation does not automatically erase previous task outputs or provider records.
